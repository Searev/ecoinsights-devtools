# [US-003] Estimated image weight gain per page and per analysis

> **Epic:** Image optimisation (US-001 → US-002 → US-003)
> **Depends on:** US-001 and US-002
> **Status:** READY FOR TDD

## 1. User Story Statement

**As an** eco-design auditor,
**I want to** see, for every page and for the whole journey, how many kilobytes could be saved by applying both image best practices,
**So that** I can quantify the expected benefit in my audit report and in the CSV export.

## 2. Context & Detailed Description

US-001 and US-002 each report a standalone gain. Their savings **overlap**: a 240 kB JPEG cannot save "180 kB by resizing" *and* "144 kB by converting". The total must therefore come from **one combined estimate per image**. This was a product decision.

### Combined estimate per image

`combinedGain = originalBytes − bytes(image resized to its US-001 target size AND encoded in its US-002 recommended format)`, clamped at 0.

| The image is… | Combined estimate |
|---|---|
| Offender of US-001 only | resized in its own format, so `combinedGain = resizeGain` |
| Offender of US-002 only | converted at its natural size, so `combinedGain = formatGain` |
| Offender of both | resized **and** converted, a third encode through the US-001 pipeline |
| Under 10 kB of combined gain, or not evaluated | 0, not counted |

### Page gain

- `pageGain = sum(combinedGain)` over the images **transferred by that page**, meaning `transferSize > 0`.
- An image served from cache on a later page doesn't count again there, because nothing was downloaded.
- It is shown as "Gain estimé (images) : 204 ko, soit 18 % du poids de la page". The percentage is relative to the page's total transferred bytes.

### Analysis gain

- The sum of the page gains, shown in the analysis summary next to the EcoIndex indicators: "Gain estimé sur le parcours : 1,2 Mo".
- Use kB under 1000 kB and MB above, with French number formatting.

### Pending and partial states

- While any image of a page is pending, the page shows "≥ 120 ko (estimation en cours)". The analysis total also shows "≥", with the same wording.
- When some images are "non évaluées", the value is displayed with "(N images non évaluées)".

### CSV export

- New column **"Gain images estimé (ko)"**, one value per page, rounded to 1 decimal.
- Pending at export time gives the current partial value, suffixed with ` (partiel)`.
- Legacy analyses give an empty cell.

### Legacy saved analyses

Analyses saved before US-001 have no gain data. They show "—" and are not counted in totals.

## 3. Tech Stack Hints & Scope Constraints

- **In scope:**
  - the combined estimate (third encode when both rules flag an image);
  - the page and analysis gain;
  - UI display in the page view and the analysis summary;
  - the CSV column;
  - persistence and migration.
- **Out of scope:**
  - converting gains into gCO2e or water. EcoIndex is not recomputed from "optimised" weights;
  - a "what-if" EcoIndex;
  - gains from other best practices (fonts, etc.).
- **State:** page gain is `pending` | `complete` | `partial` (some images not evaluated). Derive it from the per-image states of US-001 and US-002.
- **Persistence:** store per page `imageGain: { bytes, pendingCount, notEvaluatedCount }`, or the team lead's equivalent, with the analysis. `store-codec.ts` must load legacy analyses without it.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: Happy path: page gain without double counting
Given "photo.jpg" (240 kB) is an offender of both rules with a resize gain of 180 kB and a format gain of 144 kB
And resizing it to its target size and encoding it in AVIF at quality 65 is estimated at 36 kB
And no other image of the page has a gain
When the page analysis is displayed
Then the page shows "Gain estimé (images) : 204 ko"

### Scenario 2: Page gain sums images of one rule each
Given "a.jpg" is only a resize offender with a gain of 30 kB
And "b.png" is only a format offender with a gain of 20 kB
When the page analysis is displayed
Then the page gain is 50 kB

### Scenario 3: Percentage of page weight
Given a page transferred 1000 kB in total
And its image gain is 204 kB
When the page analysis is displayed
Then the page shows "soit 20 % du poids de la page"

### Scenario 4: Analysis total
Given three recorded pages with gains of 204 kB, 0 kB and 1000 kB
When the analysis summary is displayed
Then it shows "Gain estimé sur le parcours : 1,2 Mo"

### Scenario 5: Cached image on a later page is not counted twice
Given "logo.png" has a combined gain of 15 kB
And it was transferred on page 1 and served from cache (transfer size 0) on page 2
When the gains are computed
Then "logo.png" counts 15 kB on page 1 and 0 kB on page 2

### Scenario 6: Pending estimation
Given page 1 has 2 estimated images totalling 120 kB of gain and 3 pending images
When the page analysis is displayed
Then the page shows "≥ 120 ko (estimation en cours)"
And the analysis summary total is also prefixed with "≥"

### Scenario 7: Partial results after failures
Given page 1 has a gain of 80 kB and 2 images "non évaluées"
When the page analysis is displayed
Then the page shows "80 ko (2 images non évaluées)"

### Scenario 8: CSV export
Given a saved analysis with page gains of 204 kB and 0 kB
When the auditor exports the analysis to CSV
Then the CSV has a column "Gain images estimé (ko)" with values "204.0" and "0.0"

### Scenario 9: Legacy analysis
Given an analysis saved before this feature, with no gain data
When it is opened and exported
Then each page shows "—" for the image gain
And the CSV cells of "Gain images estimé (ko)" are empty
And the analysis total ignores those pages

### Scenario 10: Combined encode failure
Given an image flagged by both rules whose combined encode fails
When its combined estimate ends
Then its combined gain falls back to the larger of its resize gain and its format gain
And the page is marked "partiel"

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & Constraints

- **Sobriety:** the third, combined encode runs only for images flagged by **both** rules. It goes through the same queue, cache (keyed by URL and transformation) and caps.
- **Display:** French number formatting; one decimal for MB and none for kB in the UI. The CSV keeps one decimal in kB with a dot separator, consistent with the existing columns.
- **Accessibility:** gains are plain text, not colour-only. The pending total uses `role="status"`.
