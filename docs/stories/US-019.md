# [US-019] Ignore GIFs under 5 ko in "Limiter l'utilisation des GIFs animés"

> **Epic:** BP2 (see [EPIC-BP2-conventions.md](EPIC-BP2-conventions.md))
> **Rule:** `LimitGifs`, "Limiter l'utilisation des GIFs animés", category `images`
> **Changes:** [US-013](US-013.md). This story replaces the US-013 decision "1 × 1 tracking pixels served as GIF are counted".
> **Status:** DONE (automated gates passed, manual browser checks pending; see `docs/reviews/REVIEW-US-019-020.md`)

## 1. User Story Statement

**As an** eco-design auditor,
**I want** the GIF rule to ignore tiny GIFs such as tracking pixels,
**So that** the grade points the site owner to the GIFs worth replacing with a video or a modern format, and not to analytics beacons they often don't control and that weigh almost nothing.

## 2. Context & Detailed Description

### Size threshold

This is an owner decision.

- A GIF is **ignored** when its size is **under 5 ko**, that is **under 5 000 bytes** (1 ko = 1 000 bytes, as `formatKo` uses).
- A GIF of exactly 5 000 bytes is **counted**.
- The size is the one **already shown on the line**: the transferred size, or the decoded size when the transfer size is 0 (served from the cache). It is not the pixel size.
- A GIF whose size is unknown has both sizes at 0. It is **ignored**, as a GIF under 5 ko (PO default).
- Detection is unchanged from US-013: MIME type or extension, 2xx responses only, one per distinct URL.

### Grade

The grade is computed on **counted GIFs only**; ignored GIFs never affect it.

- **A:** no counted GIF.
- **B:** exactly 1 counted GIF.
- **C:** more than 1 counted GIF.

### Detail

This is an owner decision: ignored GIFs are mentioned, so the audit stays transparent.

| Counted | Ignored | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | `Aucun fichier GIF` (unchanged) |
| 0 | n ≥ 1 | `Aucun fichier GIF de 5 ko ou plus (n GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré(s))` |
| m ≥ 1 | 0 | `m fichier(s) GIF : à remplacer par une vidéo WebM ou MP4 pour les animations, ou un format d'image moderne` (unchanged) |
| m ≥ 1 | n ≥ 1 | the same, followed by ` (n GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré(s))` |

- Singular and plural: `1 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré`, `3 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignorés`. "GIF" itself is invariable.

### Offender lines

- Only counted GIFs get a line. Ignored GIFs have no line and no preview.
- The line format, sort order, 20-line cap and image preview (US-015) are unchanged.

## 3. Tech Stack Hints & Scope Constraints

- **In scope:** the threshold, the grade and the detail of `LimitGifs`.
- **Out of scope:**
  - detecting tracking pixels by their 1 × 1 resolution or by a list of analytics domains;
  - making the threshold configurable in the UI;
  - re-grading analyses saved earlier. Their stored grade and lines stay as saved.
- **Policy:** 5 000 bytes is a product constant, kept next to the other policy values and not inlined in the logic.
- **State:** synchronous, as before.
- **Persistence:** stored with the page, as before. There are no new persisted fields.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin Scenarios)

### Scenario 1: Happy path, the tracking pixel is ignored
Given the page downloaded "anim.gif" (900 000 bytes) and "pixel.gif" (43 bytes)
When the best practices are evaluated
Then the rule is graded "B"
And the only line is "anim.gif : 900 ko"
And the detail is "1 fichier GIF : à remplacer par une vidéo WebM ou MP4 pour les animations, ou un format d'image moderne (1 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré)"

### Scenario 2: Only tracking pixels gives A
Given the page downloaded "p1.gif" (43 bytes) and "p2.gif" (35 bytes)
When the best practices are evaluated
Then the rule is graded "A"
And the detail is "Aucun fichier GIF de 5 ko ou plus (2 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignorés)"
And there is no line

### Scenario 3: No GIF at all is unchanged
Given the page downloaded only JPEG, PNG and WebP images
When the best practices are evaluated
Then the rule is graded "A" with the detail "Aucun fichier GIF"

### Scenario 4: Boundary at 5 000 bytes
Given the page downloaded "edge.gif" (exactly 5 000 bytes) and "below.gif" (4 999 bytes)
When the best practices are evaluated
Then "edge.gif" is counted and "below.gif" is ignored
And the rule is graded "B"

### Scenario 5: Several counted GIFs, no ignored GIF
Given the page downloaded "a.gif" (45 000 bytes) and "b.gif" (12 000 bytes)
When the best practices are evaluated
Then the rule is graded "C"
And the detail is "2 fichiers GIF : à remplacer par une vidéo WebM ou MP4 pour les animations, ou un format d'image moderne", with no mention of ignored GIFs

### Scenario 6: A GIF served from the cache uses its decoded size
Given "cached.gif" has a transfer size of 0 and a decoded size of 30 000 bytes
And "tiny.gif" has a transfer size of 0 and a decoded size of 800 bytes
When the best practices are evaluated
Then "cached.gif" is counted and "tiny.gif" is ignored

### Scenario 7: A GIF of unknown size is ignored
Given "unknown.gif" has a transfer size of 0 and a decoded size of 0
When the best practices are evaluated
Then it is ignored and counted in "(1 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré)"

### Scenario 8: Duplicates and failures are still handled first
Given "pixel.gif" (43 bytes) was requested 3 times
And "missing.gif" answered 404
When the best practices are evaluated
Then the detail mentions "1 GIF de moins de 5 ko ignoré"
And "missing.gif" is neither counted nor ignored

### Scenario 9: Saved analysis is not re-graded
Given an analysis saved before this story, where "pixel.gif" (43 bytes) made the rule "C"
When the auditor opens it
Then the stored grade, detail and lines are displayed unchanged

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs) & Constraints

- **Performance:** unchanged; 2 000 requests in under 10 ms.
- **Accessibility:** no UI change other than the detail text.
